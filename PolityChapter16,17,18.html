🌾 16.1 Panchayati Raj — 73rd Amendment Act, 1992

The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992 gave constitutional status to Panchayati Raj institutions. It added Part IX to the Constitution and the 11th Schedule.

📌 Feature💬 Detail
Part AddedPart IX
Articles243 to 243O

ReservationSC/ST in proportion to population; Women — 1/3rd
Term5 years
State Election CommissionConducts Panchayat elections
State Finance CommissionConstituted every 5 years
First State to ImplementRajasthan (1959)
First State After AmendmentMadhya Pradesh (1994)
KEY POINT: States with population less than 20 lakh may not have the intermediate (block) level.
73rd Amendment = Gave constitutional status to Panchayats. 3-tier system: Village → Block → District. 11th Schedule has 29 subjects.

🌾 16.1 Panchayati Raj — Powers & 11th Schedule

⚡ Powers and Functions

  • 📋 Mandatory Functions: Agriculture, land improvement, minor irrigation, rural housing, drinking water, roads, education, health, etc.
  • 💰 Financial Powers: Can levy taxes, tolls, and fees. Receive grants from State and Central governments.
  • 🏦 State Finance Commission: Constituted every 5 years to review the financial position of Panchayats.
