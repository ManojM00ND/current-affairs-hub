📖 3.1 What Are Fundamental Duties?
🎁 Simple Meaning

Fundamental Duties = Moral responsibilities that every Indian citizen should follow.

Think of them like "good citizen rules" — they tell you what you should do to help the nation.

🔑 Terms Explained Simply

📌 Term💬 Simple Meaning
Part IV-AThe section of the Constitution where duties are written
42nd Amendment (1976)A change made in 1976 that added Fundamental Duties
Sardar Swaran Singh CommitteeThe group that recommended adding these duties
Moral obligationsThings you should do — not forced by law
Non-justiciableYou cannot be taken to court for not following them
86th Amendment (2002)Added the 11th duty — education for children
USSR ConstitutionSoviet Union's Constitution — India borrowed this idea from them
Fundamental Duties = Your responsibilities as a good citizen. You can't be punished by court for not doing them, but they remind you what you should do for India.
⚠️ IMPORTANT:
  • ✅ They apply only to citizens of India — not to foreigners
  • ❌ They are not enforceable in courts
  • 📜 Originally 10 duties → Now 11 duties

📜 3.2 The 11 Fundamental Duties (1 – 6)

1Abide by Constitution

Respect the Constitution, National Flag, and National Anthem.

2Cherish Noble Ideals

Follow the ideals that inspired the freedom struggle.

3Uphold Sovereignty

Protect the unity and integrity of India.

4Defend the Country

Render national service when called upon.

5Promote Harmony

Common brotherhood, renounce practices derogatory to women.

6Preserve Composite Culture

Value and preserve the rich heritage of India's composite culture.

🔑 Terms Explained Simply

📌 Term💬 Simple Meaning
SovereigntyIndia is independent — no outside power controls us
IntegrityKeeping India united and whole
National ServiceHelping the country when needed (like during war or disaster)
Composite CultureIndia's mixed culture — a blend of many religions and traditions
Derogatory PracticesActions that insult or disrespect women
Respect the nation. Protect unity. Help during crisis. Respect women. Preserve our mixed culture.

📜 3.2 The 11 Fundamental Duties (7 – 11)

7Protect the Environment

Protect forests, lakes, rivers, wildlife; have compassion for living creatures.

8Develop Scientific Temper

Humanism and spirit of inquiry and reform.

9Safeguard Public Property

Abjure violence.

10Strive for Excellence

In all spheres of individual and collective activity.

11Provide Education to Children

Between ages 6 and 14 years — Added by 86th Amendment, 2002

🔑 Terms Explained Simply

📌 Term💬 Simple Meaning

(2) Fundamental Rights and Fundamental Duties are complementary.
