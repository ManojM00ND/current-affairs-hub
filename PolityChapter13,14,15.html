⚖️ 13.1 The Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) — Article 148

📌 Appointment and Qualifications

📌 Feature💬 Detail
Article148
AppointmentBy the President
QualificationsNot specified in the Constitution (Convention: senior IAAS officer)
Term6 years or until age 65 years, whichever is earlier
ResignationAddressed to the President
RemovalSame as a Supreme Court Judge (Impeachment by 2/3rd majority of both Houses)
First CAG of IndiaV. Narahari Rao
Current CAG (2025)K. Sanjay Murthy

⚡ Powers and Functions of CAG

FunctionDetail
Audit of AccountsAudits all accounts of the Union and States.
Audit of PSUsAudits government companies and corporations.
Advisory RoleAdvises the President/Governor on matters of accounts.
Report SubmissionSubmits audit reports to the President/Governor, who places them before Parliament/State Legislature.
Public Accounts CommitteeAssists the PAC in examining the CAG's reports.
Compilation of AccountsCompiles and maintains accounts of the Union and States.
KEY POINT: The CAG is called the "Guardian of Public Purse" and the "Friend, Philosopher, and Guide" of the Public Accounts Committee.
CAG = The auditor of India. Checks how government spends money. Called "Guardian of Public Purse."

🏛️ 13.2 Other Constitutional Bodies

🏛️ 13.2.1 Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)

📌 Feature💬 Detail
Article315 to 323
Established1926 (Originally Federal Public Service Commission)
AppointmentBy the President
Members1 Chairman + 10 Members (maximum)
Term6 years or until age 65 years
FunctionsConducts Civil Services Examination, Engineering Services, etc.
First ChairmanSir Ross Barker

💰 13.2.2 Finance Commission

📌 Feature💬 Detail
Article280
AppointmentBy the President every 5 years
Composition1 Chairman + 4 Members
FunctionsRecommends distribution of taxes between Centre and States.
First Finance Commission1951 (Chairman: K.C. Neogy)
Current (16th) Finance CommissionChairman: Arvind Panagariya (2024)
