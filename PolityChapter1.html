🏛️ Topic 1.1: What is a Constitution?
📖 Definition

A Constitution is the supreme law of a country — a set of rules and principles that:

  • 🏗️ Determines how the government will be formed
  • ⚖️ Defines powers and limits of the government
  • 🛡️ Guarantees rights to citizens
  • 🤝 Creates a relationship between government and citizens
⚠️ KEY POINT: Every law made by the government must be in accordance with the Constitution. If it violates the Constitution → declared invalid by the courts.
💡 Quick Recap
  • Constitution = Supreme Law
  • Government powers = Limited
  • Courts = Guardian of Constitution

🏗️ Step 2: Formation of the Constituent Assembly

📌 Cabinet Mission Plan (1946) — This plan finally constituted the Constituent Assembly.

  • 🗳️ Election: Members elected by Provincial Legislative Assemblies
  • 📅 First Meeting: 9th December 1946
  • 👴 Temporary President: Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha (oldest member)
  • 👑 Permanent President: Dr. Rajendra Prasad (elected 11th Dec 1946)

⚖️ Topic 1.5: The Preamble
📜 "Soul of the Constitution"
"WE, THE PEOPLE OF INDIA, having solemnly resolved to constitute India into a SOVEREIGN SOCIALIST SECULAR DEMOCRATIC REPUBLIC..."
🏷️ Word💬 Meaning📌 Note
👑 SovereignIndependent state, no external authority—
🤝 SocialistWelfare of all, not just few✅ 42nd Amendment
🕊️ SecularNo state religion, all treated equally✅ 42nd Amendment
🗳️ DemocraticElected govt, "one person one vote"—
🏛️ RepublicElected head (President), not hereditary—
⚖️ JusticeSocial, Economic, Political—
🕊️ LibertyThought, expression, belief, faith, worship—
🤝 EqualityStatus & opportunity—
❤️ FraternityDignity + Unity & Integrity✅ "Integrity" 42nd Amendment
⚠️ IMPORTANT:
  • ❌ Not enforceable in a court of law
  • ❌ Not a source of power to the government
  • ✅ Used to interpret the Constitution when there is ambiguity
