THE PARLIAMENT

🏛️ Chapter 6 — The Supreme Legislative Body of India

✨ President • Rajya Sabha • Lok Sabha ✨
📖 6.1 What is Parliament?

The Parliament of India is the supreme legislative body of the Union. Think of it as the "law-making factory" of the country — it makes the rules that everyone in India must follow.

The Parliament consists of three parts:

  • 👑 The President — not a member of either House, but an integral part
  • 🏛️ The Rajya Sabha — Council of States (Upper House)
  • 🏛️ The Lok Sabha — House of the People (Lower House)
📜 Article 79 Says:

"There shall be a Parliament for the Union which shall consist of the President and two Houses."

Parliament = President + Rajya Sabha + Lok Sabha. Together, they make laws for India.
👑 6.2 The President and Parliament

The President is not a member of either House. But the President is an integral part of Parliament. This means the President plays a key role in how Parliament works.

👑 The President's Role in Parliament
  • 📢 Summons and prorogues both Houses (opens and closes sessions)
  • 🏛️ Dissolves the Lok Sabha
  • ✍️ Gives assent to bills (approves laws)
  • 🎭 Nominates members to both Houses
  • 📜 Promulgates Ordinances (emergency laws) when Parliament is not in session
President can't vote in Parliament, but he opens/closes sessions, approves bills, and nominates some members.

🏛️ 6.3 The Rajya Sabha (Council of States) — Article 80

The Rajya Sabha is the Upper House of Parliament. It represents the States of India. Think of it as the "house of states" — where states get a voice at the national level.

ElectionIndirectly elected by elected MLAs of States/UTs
Presiding OfficerVice-President of India (ex-officio Chairman)
Deputy ChairmanElected by Rajya Sabha members

🔑 Key Points About Rajya Sabha

  • 🏛️ Permanent House: Cannot be dissolved. Only 1/3rd of members retire every 2 years.
  • 🗳️ Elected Members: Represent States and Union Territories.
  • 🎭 Nominated Members: 12 members nominated by the President for contributions to literature, science, art, and social service.
  • ❌ Voting: Nominated members cannot vote in the election of the President.
  • 💰 Money Bills: Rajya Sabha can only make recommendations (within 14 days). It cannot reject or amend Money Bills.

⭐ Exclusive Powers of Rajya Sabha

  • 📜 Can declare a subject in the State List as a subject of national importance (Article 249)
  • 🏛️ Can create new All India Services (Article 312)
Rajya Sabha = House of States. It's permanent (never dissolved). Members serve 6 years. VP is the Chairman.

🏛️ 6.4 The Lok Sabha (House of the People) — Article 81

The Lok Sabha is the Lower House of Parliament. It represents the people of India. Think of it as the "house of the people" — where citizens get a direct voice through their elected representatives.

📊 Composition

📌 Feature💬 Detail

Term5 years

Presiding OfficerSpeaker
Deputy SpeakerElected by Lok Sabha members

🔑 Key Points About Lok Sabha

  • 🏛️ Temporary House: Can be dissolved by the President on the advice of the Prime Minister.
  • 🎙️ Speaker: The presiding officer. He/She decides whether a bill is a Money Bill.
  • 🥈 Deputy Speaker: Elected by members. Presides in the absence of the Speaker.
  • 💰 Money Bills: Can only be introduced in the Lok Sabha.
  • 🗳️ No-Confidence Motion: Can only be introduced in the Lok Sabha.
  • 🚨 National Emergency: Can be approved only by the Lok Sabha (Rajya Sabha has no role in the initial approval, though it can approve its continuation).
Lok Sabha = House of the People. It can be dissolved. Members serve 5 years. Speaker is the head. Money Bills start here.

📅 6.6 Sessions of Parliament

Parliament meets in three sessions every year. Each session has a specific purpose and timing.

📅 Session⏰ Timing💬 Duration / Note
💰 Budget SessionFebruary – MayLongest session
🌧️ Monsoon SessionJuly – September3 – 4 weeks
❄️ Winter SessionNovember – December3 – 4 weeks

🔑 Key Points

  • ⏳ The gap between two sessions cannot exceed 6 months.
  • 👑 The President summons and prorogues the sessions.
  • 📜 Prorogation: Ends a session. Pending bills (except those passed) lapse.
  • 🏛️ Dissolution: Ends the life of the Lok Sabha. All pending bills lapse.
